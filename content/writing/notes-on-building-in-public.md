Sharing work early is uncomfortable, which is mostly the point. A few things that
have helped:

1. **Ship the smallest honest version.** Not a demo of what it could be — what it is.
2. **Write down the decision, not just the result.** Future-you will want the why.
3. **Set a time box.** Polish expands to fill whatever time you give it.

## On feedback

Ask specific questions. "Thoughts?" gets you vibes; "Is the first step confusing?"
gets you something you can act on.
